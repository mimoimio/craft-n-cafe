# Player Promises

## Core promise
Build a cafe that looks like yours, run it, and watch it grow.

## First 10 minutes
Serve your first customers, discover a recipe, and buy your first upgrade.

## Day 1
Hire your first barista and expand your cafe.

## Day 7
Climb the cafe ranks and start completing your recipe book.

## Long-term
Unlock all stations, recipes, workers, and the largest cafe plot.

## Motivation levers
- Placeables: make the cafe yours.
- Expansion: visible physical growth.
- Upgrades: faster, better service.
- Completion: recipe book and station collection.
- Social: daily leaderboards and ranks.
- Group: join reward and group badge.

## Show, don't tell
- NextGoal HUD.
- Locked item silhouettes and red dots.
- Level-up reveals.
- Perfect-cup toast.
- Rank progress in the Ranks panel.

## Do not promise
- Gacha or random paid rewards.
- A second currency.
- Pay-to-win leaderboard power.
