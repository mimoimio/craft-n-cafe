## My thoughts

### 1. The Register #2 wall is fixed

At level 4 the player had enough cash for the now-300 Register #2. They bought it at **06:00** and placed it at **06:30**, with **no cash pack**.

That is exactly the desired result: capacity is reachable through normal play, and the cash pack is no longer required.

### 2. Hybrid play roughly doubled cash income

Run A earned **46.1/min** with no upgrades. Run B earned **87.5/min** with cheap ingredient upgrades and Chocolate.

Cash per customer also rose from **9.67 to 16.83**. This is not yet Session 1 levels because there was no 2x Cash and fewer/high-tier stations, but the hybrid approach clearly works better than pure capacity.

### 3. Capacity stayed strong

Customers/min rose slightly from **4.76 to 5.20**, and level 6 arrived at **20:30** instead of **37:00** in Session 1.

Register #2 + Foam + cheap upgrades is a healthy early build.

### 4. Chocolate arrived late

Chocolate was bought at **33:30**, long after level 5 and after Worker #1. The station prompt may have been visible, but the player prioritized Worker and upgrades first.

The station goal is working, but it is not strong enough to pull the player toward value early. If you want Chocolate sooner, make the next-station goal more persistent or place it before Worker in the NextGoal ordering at level 6.

### 5. Teabag and plot expansion remain unreached

This is now more about **spending priority** than a hard wall:

- The player spent **3,408** total.
- Teabag costs **2,500**.
- Plot expansion costs **3,000**.

If they had saved instead of buying Worker + upgrades, plot expansion would have been reachable. So the wall is now a meaningful choice, not an impossible gate.

That is arguably healthy. But if building is the core differentiator, the first plot expansion may still be too easy to postpone. Consider lowering the first expansion to **1,500–2,000** or making it a visible mid-game goal.

### 6. Level pacing after 6 is slow

Level 6 → 7 took **15 minutes**; level 7 → 8 took **12 minutes**. Level 9 needs another **124 EXP**.

This is not “too fast.” If anything, the mid-game can feel stalled. More customers or higher EXP per complex drink would help, but do not change the curve yet until you test a building-first run.

### 7. Worker hire is reasonable now

Worker #1 at 23:30, three minutes after level 6. Worker craft speed upgraded later. The Worker did not steal the whole session because it arrived mid-run.

With the 6s player-priority delay, the player still had time to serve manually before the worker claimed.

### 8. No passes and no cash pack confirmed

`cash_purchased_total = 0` for the whole run, and `has_double_cash = false`, `has_premium_paint = false`.

This is the first clean free-player run.
